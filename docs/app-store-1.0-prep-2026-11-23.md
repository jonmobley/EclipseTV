## New text

TV DESCRIPTION
Eclipse for Apple TV is the big-screen half of Eclipse. Put photos, videos and slideshows on your TV in full screen, with nothing else on screen.

FULLSCREEN LIBRARY
Browse your photos and videos in a clean grid made for the Siri Remote, then show any item full screen.

CONTROLLED FROM YOUR IPHONE
Pair with the free Eclipse app on iPhone or iPad over your local network using the code shown on the TV. Send photos and videos to the TV and run the show from your phone while the audience sees only what you choose.

MADE FOR LIVE EVENTS
Presentations, services, classrooms, lobbies and parties.

No ads, no tracking.

IPHONE DESCRIPTION
Eclipse puts the right thing on the big screen at live events, presentations and services, while your iPhone or iPad stays in your hand as the remote.

BUILD A SHOW
Collect photos, videos, slideshows, websites, PDFs, a live camera, a screensaver and countdown timers into a named Show, in the order you'll need them.

PRESENT ANYWHERE
Send your Show to an Apple TV, any AirPlay display or an HDMI adapter. The display shows only what you choose; your phone shows what's next.

SWITCH WITH CONFIDENCE
Tap to put any item live, fade to the screensaver between segments, and run countdowns to the start of the event.

APPLE TV RECEIVER
Pair with the free Eclipse app on Apple TV over your local network for a fullscreen library on the big screen.

LIVE CAMERA
Use the phone's camera as a live source on the display.

POLLS (OPTIONAL)
Write and run audience polls and quizzes that phones join from anywhere, no room Wi-Fi needed.

SYNC
Shows can sync between your own iPhones through iCloud.

No ads, no tracking.

TV NOTES
No account is needed. On first launch the Apple TV shows a small sample library (photos and a video). Use the Siri Remote to move around, press Select to show an item full screen, and Menu to go back. The ⋯ Options button (top right of the grid) has pairing and help.

Pairing: the TV shows a 6-digit pairing code. In the iPhone app 'Eclipse | Screen Control' (same Wi-Fi), open Settings → EclipseTV, enter the code and allow Local Network on both devices. Photos and videos tapped in a Show on the iPhone then appear full screen on the TV. The TV app also works on its own with the sample library.

IPHONE NOTES
No account is needed for the main app. To test: tap New Show, name it, open it and add photos or videos with +. Then start AirPlay from Control Center → Screen Mirroring (an Apple TV or AirPlay-capable Mac works), or connect an HDMI adapter, and tap any tile to put it on the display. The phone stays the controller; the live preview at the top mirrors what the audience sees.

No second screen? Turn on Settings → Practice Mode to rehearse a Show on the phone alone.

Optional: the companion Eclipse Apple TV app can be linked from Settings → EclipseTV using the code the TV shows (same Wi-Fi, allow Local Network on both devices).

Polls are optional and sign in with a code emailed to the polls.eclipseapp.com account, so they aren't needed to review the rest of the app. If you'd like to try them, contact us at the email above and we'll set up access right away.

Permissions: Local Network (find the Apple TV), Photos (choose media), Camera and Microphone (live camera on the display and QR pairing).
