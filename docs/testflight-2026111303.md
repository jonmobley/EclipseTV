# Eclipse build 2026111303 (iPhone and iPad only)

Built from EclipseTV branch release/testflight-20261113-3: release/testflight-20261113-2
(build 2026111302) with feature/blackout-hold (74a2b06) merged in. Everything in 2026111302 is kept.

Sibling packages, unchanged from 2026111302: eclipsepro main 8403c60f (LivePollKit,
EclipsePhoneCameraClient), CamTail main ac076aa (CamTailKit 1.8.0).

New since 2026111302:
- Blackout hold: while Blackout is on only the TV goes black. Tile taps open a private
  preview, a held video can be scrubbed silently in the top preview, and ⋯ › Show After
  Blackout cues what comes next (UP NEXT tag). Ending Blackout puts that live, or brings back
  what was live (video paused where it was left, slideshow on its slide).
- Fit / Fill toggle shows the words; Camera / PTZ tiles say "Camera Controls" / "PTZ Controls".
- Recent Shows: two per row on an upright phone.
- Hero repaints when a photo or video replaces a live countdown.

Not in this build: Apple TV. Paused return of a held video on an Apple TV needs the next
Apple TV build; until then the TV plays it when Blackout ends.
Not yet on main: all of the above, plus everything listed in 2026111302.
