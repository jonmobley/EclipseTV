  Lock and Blackout go to the first phone.
- New on that remote: the slide strip under the preview (tap a slide, swipe the preview), the slide that is on
  the TV shown in the preview, and the Fit / Fill button.
- From main: the music bar has two rows with Pause and Next (f556463).

Not on the remote yet: turning PDF pages, scrolling a website, Prompter and PTZ go-live, the live camera's
frames and capture, pinch-to-crop, a moving preview picture.

Both phones need this build: an older build ignores the new slide and Fit / Fill messages.
