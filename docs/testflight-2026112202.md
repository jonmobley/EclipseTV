# Eclipse build 2026112202 (iPhone and iPad)

Built from EclipseTV branch claude/camera-mode (branch release/testflight-2026112202),
which is main a2e0dcc (everything in 2026112201) plus Camera Mode. Not on main yet.

New since 2026112201:
- Camera Mode (Home menu > Camera Mode, or scan the Mac's Phone Camera QR code): the
  regular full-screen Camera for a volunteer running the camera for Eclipse on a Mac.
  Show controls are hidden, Back becomes Hold to Exit (returns Home), and a pill shows
  whether the picture reaches the Mac. Frames, quick-change stills, zoom, flip, photo
  and record all stay; the frame and parked still are drawn into the Mac picture.
  Survives relaunch and reconnects on its own; the screen stays awake.
- Replaces the old Send Camera to Mac screen (Settings row now opens Camera Mode).
- Links from outside the app ask before sending the camera and name the Mac; only
  Macs on the local network are accepted.
- The phone keeps one ID across launches, so the Mac no longer adds a duplicate card.

Apple TV: no change; not uploaded.

Sibling packages: eclipsepro 1c0b5e21 (branch claude/phonecam-stable-id = main 9f8accd2 plus
the stable phone ID and the frame feed for Camera Mode), CamTail ac076aa (unchanged).

Not checked before upload: real phone-to-Mac streaming, frames and stills on the Mac,
front camera orientation (simulator has no camera; unit tests and simulator UI checked).
