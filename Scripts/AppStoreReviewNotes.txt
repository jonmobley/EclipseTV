Eclipse Review Notes (iPhone/iPad 1.0)
======================================

Eclipse puts photos, videos, countdowns and slides on a big screen during
live events. The iPhone/iPad app is the controller; the screen can be an
Apple TV running Eclipse, an AirPlay receiver, or a screen plugged into the
iPhone/iPad. No account is needed for any of this.

Quickest test (one device, no Apple TV)
---------------------------------------
1. Open Eclipse on iPhone or iPad and allow Photos when asked.
2. Tap a photo or video in the library: it opens on the "Live" screen.
3. Open Control Center > Screen Mirroring and pick any AirPlay receiver
   (Apple TV or AirPlay TV). Eclipse shows the picture full screen there,
   without the controls. A USB-C/HDMI adapter works the same way.

With an Apple TV (both on the same Wi-Fi)
-----------------------------------------
1. Install Eclipse on the Apple TV and open it.
2. Open Eclipse on iPhone; allow Local Network on both devices.
3. Tap the connection pill at the top of the library and pick the Apple TV.
4. Tap a photo or video; it appears on the TV.

Features that need a second device or a Mac
-------------------------------------------
These are optional. Everything above works without them.
- Show Live: two iPhones/iPads signed in to the same iCloud account run one
  show together over the local network.
- Eclipse for Mac remote / Send Camera to Mac: pairs with the Eclipse
  app for Mac on the same Wi-Fi by scanning the QR code it shows. The app
  only connects to addresses on the local network.
- PTZ camera control: controls a network PTZ camera on the local network.
  Voice control (microphone + speech recognition) is off until the user
  turns it on in the camera controls.

---------------------

Permissions used
----------------
- Photo Library: choose photos/videos to show; save camera captures.
- Camera: live camera on the big screen, Send Camera to Mac, QR pairing.
- Microphone: record video with sound; optional PTZ voice control.
- Speech Recognition: optional PTZ voice control ("wide shot"), on device
  when supported.
- Local Network / Bonjour: find the Apple TV, Mac, other iPhones and PTZ
  cameras on the same Wi-Fi.
- Background audio: keeps a show's music or video sound playing when the
  screen locks or the operator switches apps mid-event.

Network notes
-------------
NSAllowsArbitraryLoads is set because the app talks to devices on the local
network by IP address over plain HTTP (Eclipse for Mac, PTZ cameras), which
cannot have certificates. Internet services (iCloud, QuestPoll) use HTTPS.

iCloud
------
Shows and settings sync through the user's private iCloud database
(CloudKit). Nothing is sent to our own servers except Live Poll data,
when the user uses Live Poll.

Encryption
----------
Only standard operating system encryption (HTTPS, CloudKit,
MultipeerConnectivity, CryptoKit HMAC for local pairing).
ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO.
